Article 1 (Purpose)

These terms set out what is needed to use the mobile game Natmalnyang (the "Service") provided by soarus (the "Operator").

Article 2 (What the Service offers)

  1. The Service is a word-guessing game. It can be played in Korean and 28 other languages. It offers the daily word, stages, a word collection and bookshop decorating.
  2. The Service is used without sign-up or login. The Operator does not create member accounts.
  3. The Operator does not keep a server that stores users' game records. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Article 3 (Storage and loss of records)

  1. Game records (progress, stars, streaks, bookshop, word collection, fish, hearts, time taken on a board) are stored only on the user's device.
  2. Therefore, if you delete the app or change devices, game records cannot be recovered, because the Operator keeps no separate copy.
  3. However, records of paid purchases remain in your Google Play / App Store account and are restored when you reinstall with the same account. You can restore them with "Restore purchases" in Settings.

Article 4 (Paid items)

  1. The Service sells Remove ads as a one-time purchase. Once bought, banner and full-screen ads no longer appear.
  2. Payment is processed by Google Play or the App Store. The Operator does not receive card numbers or other payment details.
  3. Prices and currency follow what the store shows.
  4. Even after buying Remove ads, rewarded ads (pressing "Watch" yourself to receive hearts or extra tries) remain available. These ads appear only when the user presses the button.

Article 5 (Withdrawal and refunds)

  1. Refunds follow the refund policies of Google Play and the App Store. Apply to the store that processed the payment.
  2. The Operator sets no refund conditions in these terms that are less favorable to users than the law or store policies.
  3. If a matter cannot be resolved through the store, you may contact us at the address in Article 13.

Article 6 (Ads)

  1. The Service includes ads. Ads are not personalized.
  2. The information processed by ad providers is described in the Privacy Policy.
  3. Watching ads is the user's choice, and you can keep playing without watching rewarded ads.

Article 7 (What users must not do)

Users must not:

  1. Reverse-engineer or modify the Service to fraudulently increase records, fish or hearts
