Natmalnyang Privacy Policy

Effective date: November ○○, 2026 (previous version: November 21, 2026 — this version adds the information kept on the server when you use the "Friends" feature, and corrects the statements about where game records are kept and about entered words)

Translation. This English text is a translation of the Korean policy, which is the original. If the two differ, the Korean text applies. 한국어 원문 →

soarus (the "Operator") runs the Natmalnyang app (the "Service") and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what information the Service processes and why.

1. Basic principles

  • Natmalnyang has no sign-up and no login. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location (GPS).
  • Your stage and Daily Challenge records, streaks, bookshop, practice records, fish and hearts are stored only on this device. The exception is a game you play in a friends room after turning on the "Friends" feature: the server scores it, so the record of that game goes to the server (section 2).
  • We look at usage statistics about how the app is used, collected without your name (section 2). We cannot tell who used the app, and the words you enter in the game are never sent. You can turn this off in Settings. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, usage statistics are off until you turn them on.
  • If you send a report from inside the app (the "I saw something strange" button, or "Report a bug" in Settings), the text you write goes to the Operator's server (section 2). One random number that identifies the device is used with it, so that we can send you a reply. No name, email address or phone number is collected here either.
  • The "Friends" feature is off until you turn it on yourself. Once on, information such as who your friends are, the name friends see (a nickname), your bookshop layout and your friends-room records is stored on the Operator's server (section 2). Friends are made only with an invitation code. The app does not read your contacts or address book, and there is no free text you type (you choose the name from a list the app provides). It is erased when you stop using the feature (section 5).
  • The only company that receives information is Google — for ads (AdMob), usage statistics (Firebase Analytics), delivery of word data (Firebase Hosting), and the Google servers (Firebase) that hold in-app reports and Friends information (sections 2 and 4).

Ads are not personalized. On both Android and iOS the app requests only non-personalized ads. On iOS the app-tracking permission prompt (ATT) is not shown.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app first shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested (section 12).

Advertising identifiers are not used for usage statistics. Your name, email address and contacts are never sent anywhere, and no words you enter in the game are sent to usage statistics. For in-app reports, only the text you write is sent. However, a game you play in a friends room is scored by the server, so the sequence of words you entered in that game goes to the server (table above). Words entered in stages and the Daily Challenge are not sent.

What friends can see. Friends can see your nickname, your bookshop layout and chosen cat, friends-room rankings and records, and attendance greetings. People who are not your friends cannot. Friends are made only by exchanging an invitation code; we do not read your contacts to find them. Because there is no text you write (you choose names from a list), you cannot put a name, school or contact detail into anything other people can see.

The random number used for in-app reports and the Friends feature only tells devices apart; it is not linked to your name, email address or phone number. If you delete the app, you can no longer see replies to reports sent with that number, and you would have to start Friends again. The Friends feature answers only after Google's verification service (Firebase App Check) has checked that the app is the genuine Natmalnyang app. Google processes that check under its own policies.

5. Deletion

Records on the device are deleted together with the app. Usage statistics are deleted automatically after 14 months. If you want them deleted earlier, ask by email (please include the "statistics number" shown in Settings). Inquiry emails are deleted 1 year after the reply.

Reports sent from the app can be deleted by you on the app's "My reports" screen (those still under review). Handled reports contain the reply and exchange code, so they cannot be deleted from the screen; they are deleted 1 year after handling. If you want them deleted earlier, ask by email.

Friends. Pressing Settings → Friends → "Stop using Friends" erases the Friends information in the table in section 2 (nickname, friend links, invitations, bookshop layout, attendance greetings, rewards to receive). Your row in rooms you joined stays without a name as "a person who left" so that other people's rankings do not break. Room records and report records are erased after the periods in section 2. Unfriending someone erases the link with that friend immediately. If you do not stop but do not use the feature for 1 year, your nickname and friend links are erased.

6. Your rights

  • Records on the device: deleted with the app. Nothing needs to be requested from the Operator.
  • Ads: In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can change your choice at any time in Settings → "Ad privacy choices". You can also buy "Remove ads" or reset the advertising identifier on your phone (section 4).
  • Usage statistics: turn them off in Settings, or request deletion by email.
  • In-app reports: view what you sent on the app's "My reports" screen and delete those still under review yourself. For handled reports, request deletion by email.
  • Friends: in the app you can unfriend, change your nickname, leave a room or stop using Friends (section 5).
  • You may request access, correction or suspension of processing by email. We answer within 10 days.

7. Children

The game itself can be played at any age. However, features that send text or records over the internet (such as in-app reports and Friends) are limited to users aged 14 or over.

The first time you try to use such a feature, the app asks for your birth year once. This answer is stored only on that device and is never sent anywhere. If you are under 14, only that feature is turned off and you can keep playing.

The reason is that Article 22-2 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act requires consent from a legal guardian before processing the personal information of children under 14. To avoid collecting a guardian's contact details or card information, the Operator chose not to process the information, instead of asking for consent.

Because the app has ads and usage statistics, it is declared in the stores as intended for ages 13 and over.

Children outside Korea. Natmalnyang is a general word game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If a birth year has been entered on the device (above), the app uses it only on that device: for users under 13 it asks Google to treat ad requests as child-directed (COPPA), and for users under 16 it marks ad requests as coming from a user under the age of consent, which turns off personalized ads, and Google's consent message is handled accordingly. The birth year itself is never sent.

If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information (for example in an in-app report or an email), email soaruskr@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Security measures

What the app exchanges over the internet is ads, usage statistics, checks for the latest version and new word data, in-app reports, and the Friends feature. All use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Usage statistics, in-app reports and Friends information are kept in Google Firebase and can be viewed only with the Operator's account. The app cannot read or write the storage directly; it can reach it only through the Operator's server.

9. Automatic collection tools

Inside the app, the ad SDK uses the advertising identifier and the statistics SDK uses the app instance ID (sections 2 and 4).

If you use in-app reports, a random number that identifies the device is created and stored in the app. It is used only to send reports, receive replies and use Friends. Cookies are not used.

When you send a report or use Friends, the server's record of the request (connecting IP, device and app type) is kept in Google Cloud logs. Google keeps this record, and it is deleted automatically after 7 days. Natmalnyang does not write names or numbers into this log separately.

Controller. soarus (contact details in section 10) is the controller of the information described in this policy.

Legal bases.

  • Ads (Google AdMob): your consent, given in Google's consent message that appears when you first open the app (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The app requests only non-personalized ads.
  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): your consent. They are off until you turn on Settings → "Send usage statistics", and you can turn them off again at any time.

  • Friends: providing the service you turned on yourself — making friends, scoring and ranking friends rooms, visiting (Art. 6(1)(b)). Keeping the record of terms acceptance and identifying inappropriate names or decorations: our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe (Art. 6(1)(f)).

  • Purchases: handled by Google Play or the App Store under their own terms.
  • Checking for the latest version and new word data (section 2): only your IP address reaches Google's server for the connection; the Operator keeps no record.

Withdrawing consent. Open Settings → "Ad privacy choices" to see Google's message again and change your choice. Turn off "Send usage statistics" to stop statistics. Withdrawing does not affect processing done before.

Your rights. You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your information, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent. Email soaruskr@gmail.com; we answer within one month. For usage statistics, include the "statistics number" shown in Settings so we can find your data. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

Transfers outside the EEA/UK. Google LLC processes information in the United States and other countries (section 4). Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Automated decisions. We make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.
