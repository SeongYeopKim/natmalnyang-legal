How to play

One puzzle a day

Everyone solves the same word. Fill the boxes within six tries. Green means the right letter in the right spot, yellow means the letter is in the word but in another spot. No sign-up, no login.

Stages with a cat

Open stages one by one along the chapter map. Each chapter has its own theme, and a boss word waits at the end of it.

Solved words become books

Each solved word puts a book on the shelf. Tap a book to see the meaning again. Use the fish you collect to decorate the bookshop.
